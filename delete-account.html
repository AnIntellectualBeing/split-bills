Data Deletion Policy

Split Bills: Group Expenses (com.anintellectualcompany.splitbills) stores all group data locally on your device without creating cloud accounts.

To permanently delete all expense logs:

Go to your phone Settings ➡️ Apps ➡️ Split Bills ➡️ Storage ➡️ Clear Storage.
