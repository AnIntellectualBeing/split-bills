Privacy Policy

Application: Split Bills: Group Expenses • Package: com.anintellectualcompany.splitmates • Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Developer: An Intellectual Company

1. Overview

Split Bills: Group Expenses is an offline-first shared expense calculator. We do not require accounts or store your personal financial data on remote servers.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

  • Offline Local Storage: All group names, participant names, and expense amounts remain strictly in your local device database.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): The app uses rewarded ads to unlock extra group slots. Google AdMob may process anonymous device advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

3. Data Deletion

You can erase all groups and expense history at any time by deleting groups in the app or clearing the app storage in your Android settings.

For questions or feedback, contact us at: anintellectualcompany.contact@gmail.com.
